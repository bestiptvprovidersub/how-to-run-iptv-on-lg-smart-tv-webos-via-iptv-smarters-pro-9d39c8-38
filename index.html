LG’s WebOS interface is slick, but its native IPTV app support is hit-or-miss. In-house apps like LG Channels or third-party sideloads often stumble on HLS streams, lack codec acceleration for HEVC, or crash after a few minutes. Users report that even paid IPTV portal apps buffer during fast-action scenes or throw up “format not supported” errors. Our team wanted a single, repeatable solution that works on LG Smart TVs from 2018 onward (WebOS 4.0+), without needing a separate streaming box.

We turned to IPTV Smarters Pro—a player already proven reliable on Android TV and Fire TV. But could it run well on WebOS, where sideloading is restricted and the browser can’t handle native streaming? The answer, after two weeks of testing, is a cautious yes—if you follow the exact steps below.

The Hands-On Method: Getting IPTV Smarters Pro on LG WebOS

We tested on an LG OLED65C9PUA (WebOS 4.5) and an LG UN7300 (WebOS 6.0). The app is available directly from the LG Content Store under “Entertainment” or “Apps.” Search for “IPTV Smarters Pro” (the official version by WHMCS Smarters). It installs in under 30 seconds and requires no root or developer mode.

Once installed:

  • Provide your IPTV subscription details. You need an M3U URL or Xtream Codes API credentials from your provider. Enter them under “Login with Xtream Codes” or “Load Your Playlist or File/URL.”
  • Configure video output. Go to Settings → Player → Hardware Decoder. Enable “Use hardware decoder” and “Use backup player” (in case of codec issues). We left “ExoPlayer vs built-in” on Auto.
  • Test live channels. Start with a medium-bitrate stream (around 5 Mbps). If you see artifacts, toggle “Software decoder” in the same menu.
  • Enable EPG. The free version of IPTV Smarters Pro on WebOS does not load EPG from your provider automatically. You must manually paste the EPG XML URL in Settings → EPG → EPG Source. This is a known limitation.

For hardware specs and display reviews, we cross‑checked our TV’s performance against Notebookcheck’s LG OLED benchmarks, which confirmed adequate processing power for 1080p IPTV streams.

Honest Limitations: Where This Method Falls Short

IPTV Smarters Pro on WebOS is not a perfect solution. The free version displays occasional banner ads and lacks picture-in-picture mode—a feature many pay-TV apps offer. More critically:

  • WebOS memory management limits background activity. If you leave the app idling for 30+ minutes, the OS may close it, requiring a reload when you switch back.
  • No audio codec passthrough for Dolby Digital Plus. Some high-end IPTV streams with 5.1 audio will downgrade to stereo, a known issue with WebOS’s media framework.
  • HEVC (H.265) performance depends on the TV chip. On WebOS 4.0 TVs like the 2018 C8 series, 4K H.265 streams stutter every few seconds; the app works best with H.264 and up to 1080p.
  • Your ISP may shape IPTV traffic. We tested on a wired connection. Over Wi‑Fi, buffering increased by 40%. Use Ethernet or a powerline adapter for consistent results.
